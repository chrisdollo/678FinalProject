Trend so far (exp 1-4):
- Deep learning helps, but unevenly. EEGNet > FBCSP (mean) and the gain is
  bigger for low performers (+0.0274 group gap). First evidence supporting
  the inefficiency hypothesis.
- ATCNet's absolute mean drops below EEGNet (likely the n_times=512 < 616
  kernel auto-scaling issue) BUT the group split sharpens dramatically:
  low gain +0.1024 vs high +0.0097 (gap +0.0927). Strongest hypothesis
  signal yet.
- MI-Mamba is broken (-0.1294 vs FBCSP across the board). Not interpretable
  as a result; needs investigation before inclusion in the paper.

  ATCNet/Mamba to see if the regression persists or it's an EEGNet-specific
  issue.

   Left          428     91     75     54

Notes:
- Mean 0.6879 is BELOW EEGNet's 0.7068 — not the expected 0.78–0.82 range.
  Likely cause: braindecode's ATCNet warned 9 times about n_times=512 being
  smaller than the 616 minimum, so it auto-scaled kernels by 0.83. The
  scaling probably hurt absolute accuracy. Worth investigating later if we
  need ATCNet to match the paper's ceiling, but it's not blocking the
  inefficiency analysis.
- Group-split signal is the strongest yet: low gain +0.1024 vs high +0.0097.
  Even a sub-optimally configured attention model helps low performers
  ~10x more than high performers in absolute accuracy gain.
- S07 again the only big regressor (-0.1111 vs FBCSP) — same direction as
  EEGNet but worse. Confirms it's not an EEGNet artefact; S07 may just be
  hard for any deep model.

   S02       0.3611    0.5347   -0.1736      -0.1806

   S04       0.4375    0.5868   -0.1493      -0.2118
   S05       0.2847    0.5208   -0.2361      -0.3958
   S06       0.3472    0.4062   -0.0590      -0.2396
   S07       0.5069    0.7847   -0.2778      -0.1667
   S08       0.6736    0.7118   -0.0382      -0.0764
   S09       0.6250    0.6944   -0.0694      -0.1215
   -------   ------    -----
   mean      0.5077    0.6370   -0.1294      -0.1803

Mean gain over FBCSP, by group:
   high performers {1, 3, 7, 8, 9}: -0.1090
   low  performers {2, 4, 5, 6}   : -0.1545
   low minus high                  : -0.0455
   verdict                         : HIGH benefit more — counter to hypothesis (Mamba LOSES vs FBCSP across the board)

Aggregate confusion matrix (rows = true, cols = pred):
                Left   Right   Feet   Tongue
   Left          338    116     84    110
   Right         107    303    117    121
   Feet           74     81    296    197
   Tongue         89     59    121    379

Notes:
- Mamba performs WORSE than FBCSP on every single subject. Mean 0.5077
  vs FBCSP 0.6370 (-0.1294) and ATCNet 0.6879 (-0.1803). This is much
  worse than the paper-expected 0.78–0.82 range — something is off.
- Possible causes (not investigated yet):
    (1) mamba-ssm version drift — the kernel needs CUDA-matched binaries.
    (2) MI_Mamba hyperparameters not tuned for n_times=512 input.
    (3) 500 epochs may be over-/under-fitting; no early stopping.
- Confusion matrix is much flatter (off-diagonal values 50-200) than the
  others' — model is closer to guessing on tongue/feet (true tongue 59,
  121, 197 going to wrong classes) than learning structure.
- IMPORTANT: when Mamba "fails", it fails MORE on low performers
  (-0.1545 vs -0.1090). Same direction we'd expect from a noisy floor —
  worth flagging when comparing to the augmented runs to see if S&R
  rescues low performers more.
- Going forward: keep this number as-is for the reproduction record;
  flag for follow-up if the paper explicitly reports Mamba > FBCSP.
